Sanjivani University welcomes learners to a world-class infrastructure designed to support their academic success. The University has invested in modern classrooms equipped with multimedia technology, high-tech laboratories, and research facilities to provide learners with the resources they need to excel in their studies and pursue their academic interests.

Sanjivani University is committed to creating a green and safe campus environment. Sustainability is a core value and the university implements eco-friendly practices throughout the campus. Lush green spaces, energy-efficient buildings, and waste management systems contribute to a sustainable and healthy environment for the entire university community.

Furthermore, Sanjivani University equips its labs with state-of-the-art equipment, placing learners at the forefront of technology and allowing them to engage in hands-on learning and research in their chosen fields.

The Registrar's Office, led by Mr. Amol Appasaheb Dhakane, prioritizes student safety and well-being. Strict safety protocols and security measures ensure a secure and nurturing environment where learners can focus on their studies. Encourages learners to explore Sanjivani University with the most of the world-class infrastructure, green campus, and state-of-the-art labs and equipment available to them.

Warm regards,

Prof. Amol A. Dhakane

Registrar, Sanjivani University
