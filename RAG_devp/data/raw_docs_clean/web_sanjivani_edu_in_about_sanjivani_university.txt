About Sanjivani University

Sanjivani University, Kopargaon, is a premier institution of higher education committed to excellence in teaching, research, and innovation.

Established under the visionary leadership of the Sanjivani Group of Institutes, the University was founded with the objective of creating a transformative learning environment that nurtures intellectual growth, professional competence, and social responsibility.

Offering a comprehensive range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across Engineering & Technology, Management, Commerce, Sciences, Pharmacy, and interdisciplinary domains, Sanjivani University ensures academic depth combined with practical relevance.

The University follows a learner-centric and outcome-based education model aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, emphasizing experiential learning, skill development, entrepreneurship, research orientation, and industry readiness.

Through strong industry partnerships, incubation and innovation centers, internships, global collaborations, and hands-on projects, students gain real-world exposure and global perspectives.

Rooted in a rich legacy of ethical values and community engagement, and supported by experienced faculty, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and a digitally enabled campus, Sanjivani University strives to develop future-ready professionals and responsible global citizens who contribute meaningfully to nation-building, sustainable development, and societal progress.
