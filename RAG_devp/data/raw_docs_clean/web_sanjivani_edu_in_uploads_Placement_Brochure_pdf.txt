Late Shankarraoji Kolhe Saheb
Former Minister,
Government of Maharashtra
The Pioneer of Socioeconomic
Transformation

Late Shri. Shankarrao Genuji Kolhe, a dedicated philanthropist,
devoted his entire life to the economic and social advancement of rural
communities through education and industrialization. Revered as a visionary,
educator, and social entrepreneur, he brought joy to millions of farmers by
establishing 23 educational institutes and twelve cooperative industries.

Recognizing the importance of quality education for the oﬀspring of
farmers, he founded the Sanjivani Rural Education Society in 1983. Under its
auspices, he introduced Polytechnic and Engineering courses. Today, the
Sanjivani Rural Education Society oversees 23 institutes spanning from
playgroup to Ph.D., including Polytechnic, Engineering, Management,
Pharmacy, Medical, Arts, Science and Commerce College, Junior colleges,
International Schools, CBSE Schools, Sainiki School and Adivashi Ashram
School. Currently, 14619 students are enrolled, supported by a team of 1495
educators and support staﬀ.

His commitment to public service was also evident through his three
terms as a Cabinet Minister in the Government of Maharashtra. In various
ministerial roles, including revenue and transport, he made substantial
contributions. He served as a Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA) for
Maharashtra from 1972 to 1999.

Sanjivani College of Engineering
(An Autonomous Institute)

Established in 1983 by the Sanjivani Rural Educational Society, Sanjivani College of
Engineering (Autonomous), Kopargaon stands as a premier technical institute in
Maharashtra's unaided sector. Under the visionary leadership of Late Shankarraoji Kolhe
Saheb, the institute was founded with a mission for societal transformation and rural
upliftment through education, training, and research. Approved by the All India Council for
Technical Education (AICTE) New Delhi, the college maintains accreditation from the
National Board of Accreditation (NBA) for various programs. With eight departments, each
led by distinguished professors renowned for their domain expertise, the college ensures
comprehensive education and research opportunities.

Industry-Aligned Placement Services: Our engineering college prioritizes career
readiness, offering comprehensive placement services tailored to the demands of the
industry. Through partnerships with leading companies and specialized training programs,
we prepare our graduates for successful careers in engineering and related ﬁelds, with the
highest package reaching an impressive 20 LPA.

Incubation Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship: Fuel your entrepreneurial
spirit at our state-of-the-art incubation center, designed to support aspiring innovators in
turning their ideas into thriving businesses. From mentorship programs to access to funding
and networking opportunities, we provide the resources needed to launch and sustain
successful ventures.

Guidance for Higher Studies, International Internships, and Global Collaborations:
Explore opportunities for further academic pursuits and international experience through
our guidance services. Whether you aspire to pursue advanced studies or gain valuable
experience through foreign internships, our dedicated advisors will assist you in navigating
these pathways, broadening your horizons and enhancing your career prospects in the
global arena. Our college has also forged Memorandums of Understanding (MoU) with 12
prestigious universities abroad, providing avenues for collaborative research, exchange
programs, and international academic initiatives.

Sanjivani College of Pharmaceutical
Education and Research (Autonomous)

Sanjivani College of Pharmaceutical Education & Research (SCPER), Kopargaon is
the ﬁrst private autonomous college in Maharashtra and 9th in India, established in the year
2004 to promote pharmaceutical education and fulﬁll the skilled manpower need in the ﬁeld
of pharmaceutical sciences. The college conducts a 4-year degree course in Pharmacy
(Intake 100) and Post Graduate Program (M. Pharmacy 15 intakes each) in the subjects of
Pharmaceutics, Quality Assurance Technique, Regulatory Affairs, Pharmaceutical
Biotechnology. College is an approved PhD Research Center afﬁliated with Savitribai Phule
Pune University, Pune.

Best Pharmacy Course Award 2024 by SPPU Pune. The college is recognized by the
Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and received Autonomous Status by UGC from 2022-23
and fulﬁlls the norms and standards laid down by them. B. Pharmacy program conducted
by the college is permanently afﬁliated with SPPU, Pune. The college is recognized by the
UGC Act of 1956 under section 2(f) & 12(B) in the year 2016. In 2015, the college was recognized
by the Department of Scientiﬁc and Industrial Research, Scientiﬁc and Industrial Research
Organizations (DSIR SIRO). National Board of Accreditation (NBA), New Delhi in 2012, 2018 and
2022 reaccredited the college UG Program for the period of three years each time. In 2021,
NBA Granted Accreditation for one year to the UG Program i.e. up 30th June 2022 and further
approval is received in 2022 up to 30th June 2025. National Assessment and Accreditation
Council (NAAC) accredited the college with grade "A" in 2017 and 2022 up to 31st December
2027. In 2022 institute has secured 101 to 125 rank in 2022. College has received research
grants of Rs. 1.68 crores plus from various funding agencies like AICTE, DST, DBT, SPPU Pune,
RGSTC etc.

PLACEMENT PROCESS
The Corporate Relations & Placement team sends placement invitation to the
companies enclosing a brief summary of the courses available at Sanjivani.
On receiving the job requirement, the CRP team announces the requirement of the
company, asking interested students to submit their resumes of marks citeria, which
The company, after short listing candidates from the application pool and eligibility
data ﬁle sent to them, sends back the list of short listed candidates to the CRPT at the
earliest.
A mutually convenient date is ﬁnalized for the campus recruitment drive.
After completion of the selection process, the company is required to furnish the ﬁnal
list of selected students preferably on the same day, or as soon as possible.
The company sends offer letters to the CRPT for handing them over to the students.
Offer acceptances (from the students) are received and sent to the company by the
CRPT.
