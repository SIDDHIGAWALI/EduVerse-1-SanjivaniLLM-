TITLE: University Overview
ORGANIZATION: Sanjivani University
SOURCE URL: https://sanjivaniuni.edu.in/

Sanjivani University is an institution of higher education established on the 40th anniversary of the Sanjivani Rural Education Society. The university emphasizes academic rigor, innovation, research, global cooperation, industry interaction, and student development. Its academic offerings include Engineering and Technology, Commerce and Management, Social Science and Humanities, School of Sciences, School of Pharmaceutical Science, School of Foreign Languages, School of Interdisciplinary Studies, undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, and global leadership programmes.

TITLE: Student Development and Infrastructure
ORGANIZATION: Sanjivani University
SOURCE URL: https://sanjivaniuni.edu.in/

Sanjivani University describes an industry-powered curriculum, Centres of Excellence, innovative maker spaces, incubation facilities, communication laboratories, foreign language certifications, industry internships and placements, modern laboratories and infrastructure, study-abroad and international twinning opportunities, student development programmes, and practical-oriented teaching. The university also highlights customized learning paths and courses aligned with Sustainable Development Goals.

TITLE: Centres of Excellence
ORGANIZATION: Sanjivani University
SOURCE URL: https://sanjivaniuni.edu.in/

The university website reports that Fortinet partnered with Sanjivani University to establish a Fortinet Centre of Excellence on August 28, 2024. It also reports the inauguration of an IBM Centre of Excellence on January 23, 2025. These initiatives are presented as supporting cybersecurity education, industry-relevant training, real-world project experience, technological skills, industry readiness, and innovation.
