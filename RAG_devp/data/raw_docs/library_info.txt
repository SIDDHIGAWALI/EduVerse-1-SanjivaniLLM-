Library Timings

The central library at Sanjivani University is open from 8:00 AM to
9:00 PM on all working days, Monday through Saturday. On Sundays and
public holidays, the library remains open from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM for
reading room access only, with book issue and return services suspended.

Book Borrowing Policy

Undergraduate students may borrow up to three books at a time for a
period of fourteen days. Postgraduate students and research scholars
may borrow up to five books for a period of thirty days.
