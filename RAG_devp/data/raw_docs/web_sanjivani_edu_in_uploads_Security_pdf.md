# Security

1. 24×7 Campus Surveillance

Continuous monitoring through CCTV cameras installed at strategic locations across the campus.

2. Trained Security Personnel

Well-trained and experienced security staff deployed round-the-clock at all entry and exit points.

3. Controlled Entry & Exit

Strict access control for students, staff, visitors, and vehicles with proper verification.

4. ID Card Verification

Mandatory identity cards for students, faculty, staff, and authorized visitors.

5. Women Safety Measures

Dedicated security arrangements ensuring a safe and secure environment for female students and staff.

6. Emergency Response System

Quick-response protocols in place for medical emergencies, fire safety, and unforeseen situations.

7. Fire Safety & Disaster Management

Fire extinguishers, alarms, evacuation plans, and periodic mock drills conducted as per safety norms.

8. Hostel Security

Separate and secure arrangements for boys' and girls' hostels with wardens and night security.

9. Vehicle & Parking Security

Regulated vehicle movement with designated parking zones and monitoring.

10. Visitor Management System

Entry allowed only after proper registration and authorization.

11. Coordination with Local Authorities

Regular coordination with local police, fire department, and medical services.

12. Anti-Ragging & Discipline Monitoring

Strict implementation of anti-ragging policies supported by surveillance and security checks.

13. Digital Security Support

Monitoring of IT infrastructure to ensure cyber safety and data protection.

14. Regular Security Audits

Periodic reviews and upgrades of security systems to meet evolving safety requirements.

15. Safe & Peaceful Learning Environment

Commitment to providing a secure, disciplined, and student-friendly campus atmosphere.
