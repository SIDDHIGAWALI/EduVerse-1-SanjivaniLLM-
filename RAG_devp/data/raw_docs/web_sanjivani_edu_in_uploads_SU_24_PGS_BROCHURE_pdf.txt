Late Shankarrao Genuji Kolhe Saheb (1929-2022) was a 
dynamic force for Maharashtra's rural development, leaving an 
indelible legacy in agriculture, public service, and community 
upliftment. Beginning his journey as an agriculturist by training, 
Saheb initially entered public service as a village headman 
(Sarpanch), eventually serving multiple terms as a member of the 
Maharashtra Legislative Assembly and as a Minister in the State 
Government. Throughout his career, he tirelessly advocated for 
farmers’ rights and championed policies aimed at improving the 
quality of life in rural communities.
 
Beyond politics, Saheb was a visionary in education and 
economic development. He was instrumental in founding numerous 
Educational Institutions and cooperative industries, driving both 
social and economic progress across rural Maharashtra. Under his 
leadership, twelve educational campuses ﬂourished, reﬂecting his 
deep commitment to education as a tool for empowerment. Saheb’s 
dedication to social progress and rural advancement continues to 
inspire countless individuals, ensuring his legacy lives on.
 
In the pursuit of excellence in education and global 
collaboration, Sanjivani University, under the leadership of its 
dedicated chairman, is committed to bringing quality international 
education to its students' doorsteps. Through strategic partnerships 
with leading Institutions worldwide, the University oﬀers diverse 
programs and opportunities for students to engage with 
International faculty, exchange ideas with peers from diﬀerent 
cultures, and gain valuable insights into global issues. Beyond the 
classroom, Sanjivani University provides state-of-the- art 
facilities and hands-on learning experiences to ensure holistic 
development and prepare students for success in today's 
competitive landscape. With a focus on equipping students with 
the knowledge, skills, and mind set needed to excel in their chosen 
ﬁelds, Sanjivani University is dedicated to building a brighter 
future for its community and the world at large.

About Sanjivani University
 
Founded in 1983, the Sanjivani Group of Institutes is a renowned educational organization oﬀering 
programs in engineering, management, pharmacy, polytechnic, and more. Known for academic excellence and 
holistic development, it stands among Maharashtra's top institutions.
 
An extension of this legacy, Sanjivani University combines innovation with interdisciplinary education. 
Through industry-driven programs, skill development, and a modern curriculum, it prepares students for global 
challenges, driven by a mission to leverage technology and science for socio-economic growth.
