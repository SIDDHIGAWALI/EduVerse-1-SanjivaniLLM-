# Notice

All students of Sanjivani University are hereby informed that the Student Development & Welfare Department is initiating the process of forming the Student Council for the academic year 2026-27. Interested students are requested to submit a written application along with the signature of respective Department SDWC coordinator, HoD and Dean of the school on or before 18th-Aug- 2026, by 05:00 PM. Applications must be submitted to the respective school coordinators and also scan the application after signature and upload on the google form https://forms.gle/ZYjXi246EY2ianZn9. The selection for various positions is based on the test (online/offline), interview process and relevant experience.

# Important Instructions for Student Council Applicants:

1. The Student Council Application Form (PDF) and Google Form link are now available on the University website. Students can access them by navigating to: Student Corner \(\rightarrow\) Student Welfare Section.
2. Application Submission: Submission of the hard copy of the duly filled and signed application form to the respective Department SDW Coordinator is mandatory. In addition, students must upload a scanned copy of the signed application form through the prescribed Google Form before the specified deadline. Both submissions are compulsory.
3. Students should submit only one application for the Student Council.
4. Previous Council Members - Students who have served as Student Council members for the previous two years in the same position/post are not eligible to apply for the same position in the current academic year. Applications submitted for the same position will not be considered.
5. Submission of the application does not guarantee selection. Applicants may be required to attend an interview, presentation, interaction, or other selection process.
6. Applicants must regularly check their registered email, WhatsApp groups/notices, and university announcements for further instructions.
7. Academic Responsibility: Student Council responsibilities must be managed without adversely affecting academic performance and attendance.
8. Final Decision: The decision of the Student Development & Welfare Cell/University authorities regarding eligibility, selection, and allocation of positions shall be final.
9. Enquiry: For any clarification regarding the application or selection process, students should contact the Student Development & Welfare Cell (SDWC).
