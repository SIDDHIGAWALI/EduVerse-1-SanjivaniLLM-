#### NSS Enrollment Notification

All the FY, SY and TY students of Sanjivani University are informed that applications are open for NSS Volunteer Enrolment for the academic year 2026-27. Interested students must fill out the NSS Enrolment Form available at the University Website → Student Corner → Student Welfare.

Enrolment will be carried out strictly on a first-come, first-served basis and reservation criteria as per the NSS circular of the Government of Maharashtra, with a maximum capacity of 100 volunteers per unit. Students are encouraged to submit their forms early to secure their enrolment.

Selected volunteers are required to meet the following commitments as per UGC norms to receive the completion certificate and earn academic credit:

- Duration: Two-year commitment to the NSS programme
- Service Hours: Minimum 240 hours of community service over two years (120 hours per year)
- Special Camp: Mandatory participation in at least one 7-day Special Camping Programme in an adopted village or urban slum

The hard copy of the NSS enrolment application form must be submitted to the NSS Coordinator, Prof. Ganesh Phopase (Mob: 9405002075), on or before the prescribed deadline.
