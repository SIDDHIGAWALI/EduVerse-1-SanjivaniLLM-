Dean's Desk (SET)

Dean's Desk (SET)

At Sanjivani University, the School of Engineering and Technology (SET) is committed to nurturing innovation, critical thinking, and technical excellence in the next generation of engineers and technologists. Our programs are designed to equip students with a strong foundation in both theoretical knowledge and practical skills, fostering an environment where curiosity meets cutting-edge technology.

With a dedicated faculty, modern infrastructure, and a curriculum aligned with industry needs, we emphasize experiential learning, interdisciplinary collaboration, and real-world problem-solving. Through our close partnerships with industry leaders, we provide our students with numerous opportunities for internships, live projects, and global exposure, ensuring they are well-prepared for the dynamic challenges of the future.

We believe in shaping not just competent professionals, but innovators and leaders who will contribute meaningfully to society and the technological advancements of tomorrow. I invite you to explore the unique offerings of our school and join us on this exciting journey of learning and growth.

Warm regards,

Dr. Kavitha Rani Paramasivan

Dean, School of Engineering and Technology
