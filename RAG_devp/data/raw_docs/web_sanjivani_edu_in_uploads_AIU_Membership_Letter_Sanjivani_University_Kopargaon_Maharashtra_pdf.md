Dear **Vice Chancellor**,

Welcome to AIU fraternity!

I am glad to inform you that the **Association of Indian Universities (AIU)** has considered the application of **Sanjivani University, At-Kopargaon, Tal-Kopargaon, Dist-Ahmednagar - 423601, Maharashtra** for the grant of membership of AIU and resolved to grant the same with effect from **13.09.2024 (Date of UGC Letter)** and placed it in the **West Zone**.

I am enclosing a copy of the conditions for granting membership, approved by the General Body of the Association at its 84$^{th}$ Annual Meeting (Business Session) held on November 14, 2009 for your perusal (Annexure-I).
