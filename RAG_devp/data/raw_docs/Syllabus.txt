Course Contents
UNIT I	Foundations of Research	9 hrs.
Foundations of Research: Meaning, Objectives, Motivation, Utility. Concept of theory, empiricism, deductive and inductive theory. Characteristics of scientific method – Understanding the language of research – Concept, Construct, Definition, Variable. Research Process Problem Identification & Formulation – Research Question – Investigation Question – Measurement Issues – Hypothesis – Qualities of a good Hypothesis –Null Hypothesis & Alternative Hypothesis. Hypothesis Testing – Logic & Importance, Introduction to Systematic Literature Review (SLR), Literature review techniques (PRISMA, Scopus search), 
UNIT II	Research Design	9 hrs.
Research Design: Concept and Importance in Research – Features of a good research design – Exploratory Research Design – concept, types and uses, Descriptive Research Designs – concept, types and uses. Experimental Design: Concept of Independent & Dependent variables. Qualitative and Quantitative Research: Qualitative research – Quantitative research – Concept of measurement, causality, generalization, replication. Merging the two approaches, Design thinking in research, AI experiment design (train-test-validation split), Reproducibility concepts.
UNIT III	Measurement	9 hrs.
Concept of measurement– what is measured? Problems in measurement in research – Validity and Reliability. Levels of measurement – Nominal, Ordinal, Interval, Ratio. Sampling: Concepts of Statistical Population, Sample, Sampling Frame, Sampling Error, Sample Size, Non Response. Characteristics of a good sample. Probability Sample – Simple Random Sample, Systematic Sample, Stratified Random Sample & Multi-stage sampling. Determining size of the sample – Practical considerations in sampling and sample size, Feature engineering basics, Data collection ethics (AI datasets), Bias in datasets.
UNIT IV	Data Analysis	9 hrs.
Data Preparation – Univariate analysis (frequency tables, bar charts, pie charts, percentages), Bivariate analysis – Cross tabulations and Chi-square test including testing hypothesis of association. Descriptive Statistics, Correlation & Regression, Hypothesis Testing- t-test ANOVA, Interpretation of Data and Paper Writing – Layout of a Research Paper, Journals in Computer Science, Impact factor of Journals, When and where to publish ? Ethical issues related to publishing, Plagiarism and Self-Plagiarism, Introduction to Machine Learning evaluation metrics Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score (Python or R)
UNIT V	AI for Research and Emerging Research Tools	9 hrs.
AI Tools for Research: Prompt Engineering, Prompt using in research , ChatGPT, Gemini, Elicit, Scite.ai, Research Rabbit, Semantic Scholar, Consensus AI, Connected Papers, GitHub Copilot, Perplexity AI, AI-based Coding Assistants, Research Automation Tools, Prompt Engineering for Research, Ethical Use of AI in Academic Research, Future Trends in AI-assisted Research.
