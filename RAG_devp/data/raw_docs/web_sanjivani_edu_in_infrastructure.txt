Infrastructure

State Of The Art Library

A rich and spacious library open from 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, housing thousands of books, journals, newspapers, and reference materials. Provides a peaceful environment for study and research.

Advance Tech-Classrooms

Advanced tech-enabled spaces for future-ready learning

Computer Laboratory

State-of-the-art computer lab with high-speed internet and latest software for skill development, online exams, and digital courses under NEP.

Sports Complex

Large playground for cricket, football, kabaddi, kho-kho, athletics, and indoor games. Gymkhana supports inter-collegiate competitions and student talent.

UniHall

Spacious TedX hall for guest lectures, workshops, debates, and cultural events.

Gymnasium

Fully equipped fitness center to help students stay active, healthy, and energized through regular physical training.

Green Clean Campus

Eco-friendly and well-maintained 6.5-acre campus with lush greenery, promoting cleanliness, environmental awareness, and overall student well-being.
