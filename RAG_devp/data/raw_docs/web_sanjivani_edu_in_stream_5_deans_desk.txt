Dr. Sarita Pawar

Dean of SPS

Welcome to the Sanjivani School of Pharmaceutical Sciences, a distinguished institution under the visionary umbrella of Sanjivani University. We are proud to be a centers of excellence committed to nurturing future pharmacists who are not only academically proficient but also ethically driven and socially responsible.

Pharmaceutical sciences play a critical role in improving global healthcare outcomes. At Sanjivani,we believe in empowering our students through a robust curriculum, hands-on research, industrial collaboration, and a learner-centric environment. Our aim is to cultivate not just competent professionals, but innovators and leaders ready to address the evolving challenges in healthcare and medicine.

Our faculty comprises experienced educators, researchers, and industry professionals who are passionate about student development. We foster an atmosphere of inquiry, creativity, and continuous learning. Through active industry partnerships and state-of-the-art infrastructure, we ensure that our students gain practical exposure that aligns with global standards.

As we move forward in this era of scientific advancement, Sanjivani School of Pharmaceutical Sciences remains steadfast in its mission to contribute to society through academic excellence, research innovation, and community engagement.

I invite you to explore our website and learn more about the programs, achievements, and opportunities that await you at Sanjivani. Join us in our journey toward making a meaningful impact in the world of healthcare.

Warm regards,

Dr. Sarita Pawar

Dean of SPS
