Student Council Structure

Student Council Structure

Introduction

The Student Council is the official representative body of students at Sanjivani University. It serves as a vital link between the student community and the University Administration. The Council aims to promote student welfare, leadership, academic excellence, co-curricular engagement, and active participation in university governance.

The Student Council provides a platform for students to express their views, address concerns, contribute to institutional development, and organize various academic, cultural, sports, social, and community outreach activities. Through collaborative efforts with the University authorities, the Council works towards creating a vibrant, inclusive, and student-centric campus environment.

Objectives of the Student Council

To represent the interests and concerns of students before the University authorities.

To promote student participation in academic, cultural, sports, research, innovation, and social activities.

To strengthen communication between students and the University administration.

To develop leadership, teamwork, and organizational skills among students.

To support and coordinate student clubs, forums, and societies.

To encourage community engagement and social responsibility.

To foster a positive and inclusive campus culture.

Functions of the Student Council

Representation

Act as the voice of students in various University committees and decision-making bodies.

Communicate student concerns, suggestions, and feedback to the administration.

Student Welfare

Address issues related to academics, facilities, student services, and campus life.

Promote initiatives for student well-being and development.

Event Management

Organize academic, technical, cultural, sports, social, and community outreach programs.

Encourage active participation of students in University activities.

Leadership Development

Provide opportunities for students to develop leadership and management skills.

Encourage responsible citizenship and ethical conduct.

Collaboration

Work closely with University authorities, faculty coordinators, clubs, forums, and student organizations.

Student Council Structure

The Student Council shall consist of:

President

Vice President

General Secretary

Treasurer

Cultural Secretary

Sports Secretary

University Magazine Secretary

NSS Secretary

NCC Secretary

Ladies Representative

School/Department Representatives

Hostel Representative (if applicable)

Club and Forum Representatives

Powers and Duties of Student Council Members

President

The President shall be the head of the Student Council and shall:

Lead the Student Council throughout the tenure.

Preside over all Student Council meetings.

Represent students before the University authorities.

Coordinate with the Vice-Chancellor, Registrar, Deans, Directors, and other officials on matters concerning students.

Supervise the functioning of all student clubs, forums, and activities.

Approve major student activities and initiatives.

Guide and monitor all Student Council members in the discharge of their duties.

Serve as the student representative on University committees as nominated.

Ensure discipline, transparency, and accountability within the Council.

Assist in the smooth conduct of Student Council elections and transition to the next Council.

Vice President

The Vice President shall:

Assist the President in all Council activities.

Perform the duties of the President in the latter's absence.

Coordinate meetings and prepare meeting agendas.

Support planning and execution of student programs.

Maintain official records and documentation of Council activities.

Coordinate with all Council members and faculty coordinators.

Assist in budget planning and resource allocation.

Monitor implementation of Council decisions.

General Secretary

The General Secretary shall:

Maintain minutes and records of all Student Council meetings.

Draft official correspondence and communications.

Coordinate communication between students and University authorities.

Maintain records of student clubs, forums, and activities.

Assist the President and Vice President in execution of Council functions.

Ensure timely circulation of notices, agendas, and reports.

Act as the approving authority in the absence of the President.

Treasurer

The Treasurer shall:

Prepare and manage the Student Council budget.

Maintain records of all financial transactions.

Monitor utilization of allocated funds.

Coordinate with the Accounts Department regarding financial matters.

Prepare expenditure statements and utilization reports.

Assist clubs and forums in budget planning.

Ensure transparency and accountability in financial management.

Prepare the Annual Student Activity Calendar in consultation with the President and Vice President.

Cultural Secretary

The Cultural Secretary shall:

Coordinate all cultural clubs and forums of the University.

Plan and organize cultural festivals, competitions, and events.

Promote student participation in inter-university cultural activities.

Coordinate publicity and promotional activities for cultural programs.

Assist in sponsorship and resource mobilization for events.

Prepare annual cultural activity plans and budgets.

Sports Secretary

The Sports Secretary shall:

Coordinate all sports and fitness-related activities.

Organize intra-university and inter-university sports events.

Encourage student participation in sports competitions.

Coordinate with the Sports Department and coaches.

Prepare sports activity plans and budgets.

Promote health, fitness, and sportsmanship among students.

University Magazine Secretary

The University Magazine Secretary shall:

Coordinate preparation and publication of the University Magazine.

Collect articles, reports, and creative content from various Schools and Departments.

Work with editorial teams and faculty coordinators.

Ensure quality, originality, and timely publication of the magazine.

Serve as Student Editor-in-Chief for University publications.

NSS Secretary

The NSS Secretary shall:

Coordinate all National Service Scheme (NSS) activities.

Promote social responsibility and community engagement.

Organize awareness campaigns, community service projects, and outreach activities.

Encourage student participation in NSS programs.

Maintain records and reports of NSS activities.

Collaborate with NSS Programme Officers and volunteers.

NCC Secretary

The NCC Secretary shall:

Coordinate all National Cadet Corps (NCC) activities.

Encourage student participation in NCC programs and camps.

Assist in maintaining discipline and leadership development initiatives.

Coordinate with NCC officers and cadets.

Support national service and patriotic activities on campus.

Tenure of the Student Council

The tenure of the Student Council shall normally be one academic year or as prescribed by the University from time to time.

Code of Conduct

All Student Council members shall:

Uphold the dignity and reputation of the University.

Maintain discipline and ethical conduct.

Promote inclusiveness, equality, and mutual respect.

Work in the best interests of the student community.

Ensure transparency and accountability in all activities.

Comply with University rules, regulations, and policies.

Conclusion

The Student Council of Sanjivani University serves as an important platform for student leadership, participation, and governance. Through active engagement, responsible leadership, and collaboration with the University administration, the Council contributes significantly to the holistic development of students and the enhancement of campus life.
