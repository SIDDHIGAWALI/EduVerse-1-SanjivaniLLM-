Examination Rules

Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be
eligible to sit for the semester end examinations. Any student falling
below this threshold must apply for condonation through the department
office at least two weeks before the examination schedule is published.

Backlog Policy

Students carrying backlog subjects from previous semesters are permitted
to appear for a maximum of two backlog papers alongside their current
semester examinations, subject to timetable clashes being resolved by
the examination cell.

Revaluation

Students who wish to apply for revaluation of their answer sheets must
submit a written application to the examination department within seven
days of the result declaration, along with the prescribed revaluation fee.
