Sonai To Taiwan Via Sanjivani

Sonai To Taiwan Via Sanjivani

Arya Subhash Jadhav

My research journey to Taiwan marks one of the most defining phases of my academic growth. I had the opportunity to complete a Research Internship at National Chung Hsing University (NCHU), Taichung, Taiwan, under the TEEP Program, where I explored the practical and research-driven aspects of Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Smart Applications.

At NCHU, I was exposed to hands-on research involving data science, visualization, and the development of ML/DL models, with a focus on real-world applications such as smart agriculture. This experience went far beyond technical learning. It taught me how to think critically, approach problems with a research mindset, collaborate in a global academic environment, and grow both personally and professionally. Taiwan’s vibrant academic ecosystem expanded my perspective and strengthened my aspiration to pursue impactful, research-oriented work.

This journey would not have been possible without the unwavering support of Sanjivani College of Engineering. Sanjivani has always given me the strength and confidence to dream beyond boundaries — encouraging me to look past limitations and believe that global opportunities are achievable with dedication and perseverance. The institution’s culture of academic excellence and global exposure played a vital role in shaping my vision.

The guidance and mentorship I received at Sanjivani helped me find the right direction and clarity to turn this dream into reality. With the right academic path and continuous encouragement, I was able to prepare myself for this international research opportunity with confidence and purpose.

Sanjivani College of Engineering has consistently empowered its students to explore the world of research, innovation, and global learning. This Taiwan internship stands as a reflection of that support system — one that nurtures ambition, provides direction, and inspires students to aim higher.

This experience in Taiwan was not just an academic milestone; it was a step toward realizing a larger vision of contributing meaningfully to the field of AI and ML. I move forward with gratitude, strengthened by the foundation provided by Sanjivani, and motivated to continue pushing boundaries in pursuit of knowledge and impact.
