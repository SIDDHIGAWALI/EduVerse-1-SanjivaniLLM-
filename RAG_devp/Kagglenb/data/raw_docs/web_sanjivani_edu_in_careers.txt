Join Sanjivani University – Build Your Career with Purpose

Sanjivani University welcomes outcome-oriented professionals who are passionate about contributing to academic excellence and institutional growth across diverse domains.

If you are eligible and interested in becoming a part of our dynamic team, kindly share your detailed CV at joinus@sanjivani.org.in.

(Please mention “Application for the – <Department/School>” in the subject line.)

Career opportunities at Sanjivani University are regularly offered across various schools, departments, and administrative units, with recruitment managed by the Human Resources Department throughout the year.

Sanjivani University takes pride in fostering an inclusive work environment and offers competitive benefits, contributing meaningfully to the upliftment of all sections of society.

Join the Sanjivani Family!

We are always looking for talented, passionate, and dedicated individuals to be a part of Sanjivani University and Sanjivani Group of Institutes.
