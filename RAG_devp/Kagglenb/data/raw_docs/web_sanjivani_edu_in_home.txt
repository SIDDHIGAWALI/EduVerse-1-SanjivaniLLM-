Sanjivani University Hosts "Zdravstvuyte Russia (Namaste Russia)" to Strengthen Global Academic Collaborations.

Sanjivani University has successfully organized "Zdravstvuyte Russia (Namaste Russia)", an international engagement program aimed at introducing students to academic, research, and career opportunities in the Russian Federation.

The university had the privilege of welcoming Mr. Matvai Fetisov (Vice Consul General of the Russian Federation), Mr. Eugene (Head of Administration, Consulate of the Russian Federation) and Mr. Shivendrasinh Chauhan (Strategic Lead – Political & Institutional Alliance, AP Globale) as the Chief Guests. The dignitaries were welcomed by Hon'ble Shri. Amitdada Kolhe, President, Sanjivani University, Dr. A.G. Thakur, Vice Chancellor, and Dr. R.S. Shendge, Head of Academic Governance.

During the session, Mr. Matvai Fetisov shared valuable insights into the strengthening strategic partnership between India and Russia, highlighting opportunities in higher education, research collaborations, industrial partnerships, innovation, and global employment. His address encouraged students to broaden their global perspective and explore international pathways for academic and professional growth.

The event reflected Sanjivani University's commitment to fostering meaningful international collaborations and providing students with global exposure through interactions with distinguished diplomats, industry leaders, and academic experts.

The university expresses its sincere gratitude to the esteemed guests for their inspiring presence and acknowledges the dedicated efforts of Dr. Sudhanshu Bhatt (Director – University Relations), Prof. Amol Dhakane (Registrar), Dr. Mahendra Gawali  (Dean – SIRD) and the entire organizing team for making the event a grand success.

Sanjivani University Signs MoU with LinkedIn Learning | 15,000+ Courses for Students

Sanjivani University Signs MoU with LinkedIn Learning | 15,000+ Courses for Students

Sanjivani University has signed a strategic Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with LinkedIn Learning, unlocking access to 15,000+ online courses for its students across emerging technologies and professional domains.

Through this collaboration, students will gain access to industry-relevant learning, globally recognized certifications, and opportunities to strengthen their professional profiles and employability.

The MoU was signed in the presence of Hon’ble President Shri. Amitdada Kolhe, Registrar Mr. Amol Dhakane, Dr. Vinod Malkar, Dean, School of Online Education & Distance Learning, and Dr. Kavitha Rani, Dean, School of Engineering & Technology. The LinkedIn Learning delegation included Mr. Amit Mukherjee, Mr. Shivprasad Longani, and Mr. Anup Menon.

As part of Sankalp 2026, LinkedIn Learning representatives also engaged with first-year students, highlighting the importance of professional networking, continuous upskilling, certifications, and building a strong LinkedIn presence from the beginning of their academic journey.

This collaboration reflects Sanjivani University’s commitment to digital transformation, lifelong learning, industry readiness, and globally benchmarked education—empowering students with the skills and confidence to thrive in a rapidly evolving world.

Smart Kopargaon Hackathon 2026

Smart Kopargaon Hackathon 2026

Smart Kopargaon Hackathon 2026 is Sanjivani University's flagship national-level innovation challenge, bringing together students from across the country to develop technology-driven solutions for real-world problems aligned with Smart Kopargaon initiatives.

Highlights:

* Three-stage hybrid hackathon (Online + Offline)

* Team of 4 members

* Around 50 real-world problem statements

* Evaluation by academic and industry experts

* Dedicated mentorship throughout the competition

* Prize Pool: ₹1,00,000

Registration Opens: 5 July 2026

The Top 100 shortlisted teams will advance to the 30-hour Grand Finale, scheduled for *29–30 August 2026 at *Sanjivani University, Kopargaon, where they will showcase their innovative prototypes before an expert jury.

This is an opportunity to solve real-world challenges, strengthen your technical and problem-solving skills, collaborate with talented innovators, and compete for national recognition.
