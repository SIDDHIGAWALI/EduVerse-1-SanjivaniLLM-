How To Apply

Admission Process

Follow these carefully structured steps to complete your admission process. Each step is essential to ensure a smooth transition into our academic community.

01

STEP

Online Application

Visit our official website www.sanjivani.edu.in to begin your application process.

02

STEP

Complete Application Form

Fill out the online application form with accurate personal, academic, and contact information.

03

STEP

CET Registration

Register for Common Entrance Test (CET) if applicable to your chosen program of study.

04

STEP

Application Fee Payment

Submit the non-refundable application processing fee. ₹1,000/- Only

05

STEP

Entrance Examination

Appear for the entrance test on the scheduled date. Ensure you bring valid identification and the admit card.

06

STEP

Merit List Publication

Check the published merit list on the official website to view your ranking and eligibility status.

07

STEP

Provisional Offer Letter

Receive your provisional admission offer letter based on merit ranking. This will be sent to your registered email address.

08

STEP

Admission Confirmation

Confirm your admission by paying the first installment of the program fees within the stipulated deadline.

09

STEP

Document Verification

Submit all required original documents for verification along with attested photocopies as per the checklist provided.

10

STEP

Final Admission Letter

Receive your official admission confirmation letter after successful verification of all documents and fee payment.

11

STEP

Student Credentials

Collect your official student ID card and university uniform from the designated department.

12

STEP

Commencement of Classes

Attend the orientation program and begin your academic journey as per the academic calendar schedule.
