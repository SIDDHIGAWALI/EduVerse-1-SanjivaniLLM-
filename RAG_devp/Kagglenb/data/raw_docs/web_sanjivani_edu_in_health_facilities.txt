Health Facilities

Health Facilities

Sanjivani University provides several facilities to support Divyangjan (specially-abled) students. These include a wheelchair, ambulance, and battery-operated vehicle for easy movement within the campus.

The university also has an on-campus clinic that operates at specified times to provide basic medical care. An ambulance is available to transport patients to hospitals during medical emergencies. It is equipped with medical tools and staffed by trained personnel to provide care on the way to the hospital. The ambulance has flashing lights and sirens to ensure a quick and safe journey.
