Late Shankarraoji Kolhe Saheb

Former Minister,
Government of Maharashtra

Late Shri. Shankarrao Genuji Kolhe, a dedicated philanthropist, devoted his entire life to the economic and social advancement of rural communities through education and industrialization. Revered as a visionary, educator, and social entrepreneur, he brought joy to millions of farmers by establishing 23 educational institutes and twelve cooperative industries.

Recognizing the importance of quality education for the offspring of farmers, he founded the Sanjivani Rural Education Society in 1983. Under its auspices, he introduced Polytechnic and Engineering courses. Today, the Sanjivani Rural Education Society oversees 23 institutes spanning from playgroup to Ph.D., including Polytechnic, Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Medical, Arts, Science and Commerce College, Junior colleges, International Schools, CBSE Schools, Sainiki School and Adivashi Ashram School. Currently, 14619 students are enrolled, supported by a team of 1495 educators and support staff.

His commitment to public service was also evident through his three terms as a Cabinet Minister in the Government of Maharashtra. In various ministerial roles, including revenue and transport, he made substantial contributions. He served as a Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA) for Maharashtra from 1972 to 1999.
