Industry Connections

Connecting Campus to Corporate World

Sanjivani University maintains active collaborations with top technology and industry leaders to deliver practical education, industry certifications, internships, live projects, expert sessions and strong placement support.

Through dedicated Centres of Excellence, joint training programs and direct industry engagement, students gain exposure to current technologies and work practices — leading to high-value placements (highest up to 32+ LPA) in reputed companies.

What Our Partnerships Deliver

Centres of Excellence

Dedicated infrastructure & labs (IBM – 2025, Fortinet – 2024, Zoho – 2024) for hands-on learning in AI, cybersecurity, cloud & business applications.

Global Certifications

Industry-recognized credentials in emerging technologies, digital skills, cybersecurity, fintech and software solutions.

Internships & Live Projects

Real company projects, summer internships and collaborative development work with industry mentors.

Industry Expert Sessions

Frequent guest lectures, technical workshops, seminars and career guidance from practicing professionals.

Powerful Placements

Direct campus recruitment, high salary offers and career opportunities in leading MNCs and startups.

Innovation Support

Incubation facilities, startup mentoring and innovation challenges aligned with current industry trends.
