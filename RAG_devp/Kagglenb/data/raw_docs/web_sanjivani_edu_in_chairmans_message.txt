Chairman’s Message

Hon'ble Shri. Nitinrao Shankarrao Kolhe

Chairman, SRES

In the pursuit of excellence in education and global collaboration, Sanjivani University, under the leadership of its dedicated chairman, is committed to bringing quality international education to its students’ doorsteps. Through strategic partnerships with leading institutions worldwide, the university offers diverse programs and opportunities for students to engage with international faculty, exchange ideas with peers from different cultures, and gain valuable insights into global issues.

Beyond the classroom, Sanjivani University provides state-of-the-art facilities and hands-on learning experiences to ensure holistic development and prepare students for success in today's competitive landscape. Embracing a vision of inclusivity and empowerment, the university fosters an environment where learning knows no boundaries and where students from diverse backgrounds can thrive.

With a focus on equipping students with the knowledge, skills, and mindset needed to excel in their chosen fields, Sanjivani University is dedicated to building a brighter future for its community and the world at large.

Warm regards,

Hon'ble Shri. Nitinrao Shankarrao Kolhe
