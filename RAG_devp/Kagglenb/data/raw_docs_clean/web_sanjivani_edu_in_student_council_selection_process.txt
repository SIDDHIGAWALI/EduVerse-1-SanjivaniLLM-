Student Council Selection Process

Phase 1: Notification

Student Development & Welfare Cell issues a notification regarding Student Council formation.

Eligibility criteria, posts, and timelines are communicated to all students.

Phase 2: Application/Nomination

Interested students submit nomination forms for the desired post.

Faculty recommendations and academic records may be considered.

Phase 3: Eligibility Verification

Applicants must satisfy:

Good academic standing.

Minimum attendance as per university norms.

No disciplinary action pending against them.

Active participation in curricular/co-curricular activities.

Phase 4: Screening & Presentation

Shortlisted candidates present their vision, leadership qualities, and proposed activities before the selection committee.

Interaction/interview may be conducted.

Phase 5: Selection/Election

Depending upon university policy:

Selection Method: Committee constituted by the University selects suitable candidates.

Phase 6: Declaration of Results

Selected candidates are officially announced.

Appointment letters/certificates are issued.

Phase 7: Orientation & Oath Taking

Student Council members undergo orientation regarding their roles and responsibilities.

Oath-taking ceremony is conducted.
