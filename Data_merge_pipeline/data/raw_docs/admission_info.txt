Admission Process

Admission to the B.Tech program at Sanjivani University is conducted
through the centralized admission process governed by the Directorate
of Technical Education, Maharashtra. Candidates must have passed HSC
with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects.

Fee Structure

The annual tuition fee for the B.Tech program is revised each academic
year as per the Fee Regulating Authority notification and is payable in
two installments, one at the start of each semester.
